MU regime model backtest

345 out-of-sample predictions from 2025-04-09 to 2026-10-02, tenor 1m, earnings excluded. The model is refitted every 5 observations using only earlier data, then predicts the next change in fixed-strike vol given the spot move.

On up days the model explains 7% of the vol-change variance and gets the direction right 58% of the time (moves of 1% or more); on down days -1% and 54%.
