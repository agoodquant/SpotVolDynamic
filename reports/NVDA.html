β is the change in implied vol, in vol points, per 1% spot move for the same expiry. Fixed-strike β is zero if the smile simply stays put (sticky strike); ATM β then equals the skew slope. A fixed-strike β well above zero is vol moving with spot beyond anything the skew prices.

The short memory, half-life 20 observations model implies β up = +0.15 and β down = -0.11 for the next observation. Both are slopes of fixed-strike vol on the spot return, in vol points per 1%: a positive β up means vol rises on a rally, a negative β down means vol rises on a sell-off.
